01 / THE PROBLEM

Your memory is a highlight reel.

"I'm a 60% win rate trader."

"That setup is my bread and butter."

CHoCH: 11 trades, profit factor 0.8. It doesn't pay for itself.

"I only lose on news days."

Four of your five biggest losses are tagged "revenge" — by you, in the moment.

None of this is new information. It's sitting in your trading history right now — you've just never forced yourself to read it.

02 / THE TOOLSET

What you actually get.

  1. 01

    A log you'll actually keep

    Pair, date, session, result, R:R, P&L, screenshot, notes. Twenty seconds per trade. CSV export whenever you want your data out.

  2. 02

    Stats that can't flatter you

    Win rate, profit factor (gross profit ÷ gross loss), average R:R, monthly P&L. Same formula every time, no good-day rounding.

  3. 03

    Your setups, your tags

    Type any tag you want — BOS, CHoCH, discount, confirmation entry. Then sort by tag and see which ones are genuinely worth taking.

  4. 04

    A calendar with red days

    Daily P&L, colour-coded. Click any day and get exactly the trades you took — including the one you'd rather forget.

  5. 05

    A price on your bad habits

    Tag revenge, FOMO and over-leveraging while they're happening. The dashboard totals what each one costs you, in dollars.

  6. 06

    A month you can hand to someone

    One read-only link to your monthly report. Mentor, trading buddy, or an archive for the version of you that's starting again in January.
